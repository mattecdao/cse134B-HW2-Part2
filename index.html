GeneSite - Matthew's Site!

First of all, why GeneSite?

GeneSite is the name resulting from my common username in video games I play, geneman. It originated from the game Team Fortress 2 that I used to play in my childhood/teen years. One of the character's voiceline involved the word "gentlemen and when I was interested in learning German, Mann is the word for man. Shortened, I somehow came to the name geneman and just used it in any game I played. If it was taken, I used some other alternative for it like 'genemannen"
